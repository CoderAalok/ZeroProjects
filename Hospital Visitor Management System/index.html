About This System

The Hospital Visitor Management System helps the hospital register, verify, approve, and track visitor access securely.

  • Mobile OTP verification protects visitor identity.
  • Digital QR passes enable quick entry verification.
  • Staff and patients see only the information relevant to their role.

Help & Visiting Policy

How to request a visit

  1. Register with your name and 10-digit mobile number.
  2. Verify the one-time password sent to your phone.
  3. Select the patient and an available time slot.
  4. Show the approved digital pass at the visitor desk.

Visiting rules

  • Standard visiting hours are 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
  • Visits are limited to 60 minutes unless staff approve otherwise.
  • Ward capacity limits and staff instructions must be followed.
  • For urgent assistance, contact the Visitor Management Desk.
